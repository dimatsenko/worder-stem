This Privacy Policy describes how Worder ("the App", "we", "us") handles your information. Worder is developed and operated by Dmytro Matsenko, an individual developer ("the Developer").

The short version: your flashcards, study progress, and settings are stored locally. If you choose “Back Up to iCloud,” a copy is stored in your private CloudKit database. Optional Premium and AI features send specific text through our backend to Google. We use RevenueCat for subscription functionality and to measure Premium-offer variants, as described below. We don't run ads, don't track you across apps or websites, and don't sell your data.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, and have no access to:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email address, phone number) — the App does not require an account or sign-in;
  • Your flashcards, decks, study history, streaks, or learning statistics, except when you choose to create the iCloud backup described below;
  • Advertising identifiers, behavioral profiles, or analytics of your study activity or other App use outside of purchases and Premium-offer impressions described in Section 10;
  • Location data;
  • Your photo library or contacts.

The App contains no advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking SDKs, cookies, or fingerprinting. We use RevenueCat as a subscription-management and Premium-offer measurement provider; it is not used for advertising or to track you across apps or websites.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All App content — your decks, cards, study progress, spaced-repetition schedules, settings, and activity history — is stored locally on your device by default using Apple's on-device data frameworks. Local data:

  • doesn't leave your device unless you use a feature described in this Policy;
  • may be included in device backups (for example, iCloud Backup or local backups) if you enable them — those backups are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy;
  • is generally deleted when you delete the App, except for a separate CloudKit backup that you created.

Manual iCloud Backup (CloudKit)

If you tap “Back Up to iCloud” in Settings, the App uploads one current copy of your cards, decks, progress, statistics, and the settings listed in the App to a private CloudKit database associated with your Apple Account. This isn't automatic sync: a backup is created only after your explicit action. Apple stores it in your iCloud account under Apple's Privacy Policy. We cannot access its contents. Deleting the App doesn't necessarily delete this backup. You can delete it at any time with “Delete iCloud Backup” in the App's Settings; this doesn't delete local data on your device.

3. AI Deck Generation (Google Gemini, via our backend)

The App offers optional AI-powered features — generating a deck of words for a topic, or extracting vocabulary from text or a document you provide.

Unlike some apps, this does not require you to supply your own API key. Instead:

  • The text you submit (a topic, a list of words to avoid duplicating, or text extracted from an imported file) is sent from your device to a backend we operate (a Cloudflare Worker), which forwards it to Google's Gemini API using our own credentials and returns the generated deck to you.
  • We do not log, store, or retain the content of your prompts or the AI's responses on our backend. The request passes through in memory to generate a response and is not written to any database.
  • To prevent abuse of this service (and keep it free to offer), each request is authenticated using Apple's App Attest, a cryptographic proof that the request comes from a genuine, untampered copy of the App on a genuine Apple device — see Section 6 for details. Basic usage counters (not your content) are kept temporarily to enforce fair-use limits.
  • Google's processing of the text it receives is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and the Gemini API Terms of Service.
  • Please do not include personal or sensitive information (health data, financial details, other people's private information) in topics or text you submit for AI generation.
  • We may apply basic automated filtering to block clearly inappropriate topics before they are sent to Gemini, in addition to Google's own content-safety filtering.

If you never use the AI features, no data is sent to Google.

4. HD Neural Voices — Text-to-Speech (Google Cloud Text-to-Speech, Premium only, via our backend)

Premium subscribers can enable higher-quality neural voice pronunciation. When this feature is used:

  • The word or example sentence you are studying is sent from your device to our backend, which forwards it to Google Cloud Text-to-Speech to synthesize audio, authenticated the same way as in Section 3 (App Attest, no personal identifiers).
  • The resulting audio is cached on your device (in the App's local Caches folder) so the same word doesn't need to be re-fetched.
  • We do not log or retain the text sent for speech synthesis on our backend.
  • Google's processing of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Cloud Privacy Notice.
  • If you are offline, are not a Premium subscriber, or the network request fails, the App automatically falls back to Apple's on-device speech synthesizer described in Section 5 — no data leaves your device in that case.

5. Standard Pronunciation — On-Device Text-to-Speech (Apple)

For non-Premium users, and as a fallback for Premium users, word pronunciation uses Apple's built-in, on-device speech synthesizer (AVSpeechSynthesizer). This runs entirely on your device; no audio or text is sent anywhere.

6. Device Verification (App Attest) and Anti-Abuse

To keep the AI and voice features listed above free from abuse (which would make them unsustainable to offer), the App uses Apple's App Attest framework:

  • Your device generates a cryptographic key pair inside its Secure Enclave. Apple attests that this key belongs to a genuine copy of the App running on genuine Apple hardware, and our backend stores the resulting public key together with a randomly generated key identifier.
  • This key identifier is not your Apple ID, is not an advertising identifier, and cannot be used by us or anyone else to identify you personally or to track you across apps or websites.
  • We use this key identifier only to enforce fair-use rate limits (a maximum number of AI/voice requests per hour and per day) and to reject requests that don't carry a valid attestation.
  • Rate-limit counters are automatically deleted after 1 hour (hourly limits) or 24 hours (daily limits). The device attestation record itself (public key and identifier) is retained for as long as needed to keep the anti-abuse system working, and is replaced if your device re-attests.

7. Camera and Photo Import (On-Device OCR)

The App can turn a photo of your notes, a textbook page, or a document into a flashcard deck:

  • If you take a photo, the App requests camera access for this single purpose (see the permission prompt for the exact wording).
  • If you choose a photo or file from your library, the App uses Apple's privacy-preserving picker, which only gives the App access to the specific items you select — never your full photo library.
  • Text recognition (OCR) is performed entirely on your device using Apple's on-device Vision framework. The image itself is never uploaded anywhere by us.
  • If you then choose to turn the extracted text into an AI-generated deck, that text is sent to our backend as described in Section 3. If you only import it directly (without AI), the text never leaves your device.

8. Files You Import

If you import words from files (CSV, JSON, TXT, PDF, DOCX, RTF, DOC, or XLSX), the files are read and parsed entirely on your device. If you use AI-assisted import to turn extracted text into a deck, that text is sent to our backend as described in Section 3.

9. Notifications

If you enable study reminders, the App schedules local notifications on your device using Apple's on-device notification scheduler. This happens entirely on-device; we do not use push notifications, and no notification data is sent to us or to any third party.

10. Purchases and Subscriptions

The App offers optional auto-renewing Premium subscriptions (monthly and yearly) that unlock additional features. All payments are processed by Apple through the App Store's In-App Purchase system (StoreKit).

  • We do not receive or store your payment card details, billing address, or other payment information — Apple handles all payment processing.
  • Apple may provide us with anonymized, aggregated transaction information (e.g., number of subscriptions sold) that does not identify you personally.
  • Your subscription status is verified on-device via StoreKit. For subscription functionality, purchase-history synchronization, and measurement of Premium-offer impressions, the App also sends RevenueCat a pseudonymous app user identifier, purchase data and Premium entitlement status, and Premium-offer impression events. We do not send RevenueCat your name, email address, payment data, cards, or study progress.
  • RevenueCat processes this data for App Functionality and subscription analytics, not for advertising or cross-app tracking. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy for details.
  • Apple's handling of your payment data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

12. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has submitted personal information through the optional AI or voice features, please contact us so we can advise on further steps; note that we do not retain AI/voice request content as described in Sections 3 and 4.

13. Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA, and Similar Laws)

Under data protection laws such as the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing.

  • Your App content (decks, cards, progress, settings) is under your control on your device. Delete it in the App or delete the App to remove local content. If you created a CloudKit backup, delete it separately in the App's Settings.
  • AI/voice request content is not retained by us, so there is generally nothing to access or delete on our side.
  • Device attestation/rate-limit data is a pseudonymous, device-bound technical identifier. Hourly and daily counters expire automatically; the attestation record is retained until re-attestation or for as long as needed for anti-abuse protection. Because we don't link it to contact information or an account, we generally can't connect it to a specific person for an individual request.

We do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA. For data processed by Apple, Google, or RevenueCat, you may also exercise your rights directly with those companies.

14. Data Security

App content is stored within the App's sandboxed container, protected by iOS security mechanisms. Device attestation keys live in your device's Secure Enclave and never leave it. All network communication with our backend and with Google is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.

15. International Users

Local content doesn't leave your device without your action. If you create a CloudKit backup, use AI or Premium voice features, or receive Premium offers, the relevant data may be processed by Apple, Google, Cloudflare, and RevenueCat on servers in countries other than your own, under those companies' respective privacy and data-transfer safeguards.
